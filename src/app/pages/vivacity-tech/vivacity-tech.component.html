The company & the product

A device is only useful
when it’s ready for learning.

That’s where Vivacity comes in.

For school IT teams, buying a Chromebook is just the beginning. Deployment, inventory, repairs, and replacement all need to work together. Vivacity brings hardware, software, repair services, and training into one partnership.

Dream connects that work. It helps teams understand their fleet, coordinate repairs, and put device data to use. My team builds and operates the software behind that experience.

And the mission extends beyond technology: as a public benefit corporation, Vivacity commits a portion of its profits to giving back to schools.

My contributions → real product value

Behind the experience,
engineering that makes it possible.

As Senior Full Stack Developer & Tech Lead, I work from architecture through production. Here’s how that work connects to the platform and the people using it.

01 / FLEET VISIBILITY

Millions of syncs.
A clearer picture of every fleet.

I designed and implemented a major Google telemetry synchronization capability supporting approximately two million daily syncs across four million managed devices.

Why it matters for Dream: fleet insights depend on the data behind them. This capability provides a scalable foundation for bringing Google device telemetry into the platform.

02 / ROOM TO GROW

A growing platform.
A deliberate approach to cost.

I led the migration from Render to Google Cloud Platform, improving scalability while maintaining infrastructure costs. I architect and operate a distributed platform of approximately 50 services and five databases, with monitoring and service-level objectives guiding reliability.

Why it matters for Vivacity: infrastructure that can support growth, with cost and operational readiness built into the decisions.

03 / CONNECTED OPERATIONS

Enterprise systems.
Connected to the customer experience.

I designed the database architecture and implemented the majority of Dream’s API functionality for the NetSuite-to-Dream integration, enabling enterprise data synchronization through middleware services.

Why it matters for Vivacity: a connection between business systems and Dream, supporting the coordinated operations behind the customer platform.
